Preprocessing steps
------------------------
Case folding means converting all text to a single case (usually lowercase) so that comparisons and tokenization are consistent.
Stopword Removal- Stopwords are common words like “the”, “is”, “a”, etc., that often don’t carry much meaning.
Stemming chops words to their root form (roughly, without caring if it’s a real word).
Lemmatization reduces words to their dictionary base form (“lemma”), considering grammar and context.
Part-of-speech tagging assigns a grammatical category (like noun, verb, adjective, etc.) to each word in a sentence.
Named Entity Recognition (NER) identifies and classifies key information (“entities”) in text into predefined categories such as:
	PERSON → names of people
	ORG → organizations
	GPE → countries, cities
	DATE → dates or times
	MONEY → monetary values
Parsing
	Constituency Parsing - Groups words into nested phrases (noun phrase, verb phrase)
	Dependency Parsing - Identifies head–dependent relationships between words
