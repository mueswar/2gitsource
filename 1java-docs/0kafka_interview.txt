What is a consumer group?
	Each partition gets assigned to one consumer within that group.
	But another consumer group can independently consume the same topic
Can two consumers in the same consumer group consume the same partition simultaneously?
	Normally no. A partition is assigned to only one consumer within a consumer group at a time.
